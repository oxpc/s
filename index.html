Supported formats

Number the questions or not. Options can be lettered (A. A) (A), up to H) or just plain lines, one per line.

1. Which property sets the R class package?
A. applicationId
B. namespace
C. versionName
D. minSdk
E. packageName
Answer: B. Optional explanation here.
2. Which one is a build-time setting?
applicationId
namespace
Answer: namespace

Plain lines: the first line is the question and every line after it is an option. A blank line starts the next question, and the answer line is optional.

The answer can be a letter, a number, or the option text. You can also mark the right option with ✓, or put a key at the end such as “1. B 2. C”. Questions without an answer still work, but they are not scored.

Everything runs in your browser. Your questions and files are not uploaded anywhere.
